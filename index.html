About EcoWonders Foundation

EcoWonders Foundation is a dynamic Environmental NGO dedicated to fostering environmental sustainability, promoting education and health support, advancing green and renewable energy initiatives, raising climate change awareness, and implementing effective waste management and recycling programs. Founded in Ghana, we work hand-in-hand with local communities, students, and global partners to create lasting positive impact.

Through tree planting campaigns, clean energy solutions, environmental education in schools, and sustainable livelihood programs, we have already impacted thousands of lives and hectares of land. Join us in building a greener, healthier, and more sustainable future.
