About Us

Our story, mission, and the people driving change

Our Mission

To protect Ghana’s environment and empower communities through education, renewable energy, reforestation, and sustainable waste management.

Our Vision

A greener, healthier Ghana where every community thrives in harmony with nature and future generations inherit a sustainable planet.

Our Values

Integrity, community partnership, innovation, environmental justice, and measurable impact guide everything we do.

Our Story

EcoWonders Foundation was founded in Takoradi, Ghana, by a group of passionate environmentalists and students who saw the urgent need for practical, community-driven solutions to climate change, deforestation, and waste pollution.

What began as small tree-planting drives and school awareness campaigns has grown into a multi-faceted NGO working across the Western Region and beyond. Today we partner with local communities, technical universities, government agencies, and international organizations to deliver lasting change.

We believe that real environmental progress starts at the grassroots — with the people who live closest to the land and the sea. Every project we design is rooted in local knowledge and built for long-term ownership by the communities we serve.
